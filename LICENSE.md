## SPDX

The machine-readable expression for the choice above is:

```
SPDX-License-Identifier: MIT OR Apache-2.0 OR GPL-2.0-only OR GPL-3.0-only OR BSD-3-Clause OR OGL-UK-3.0 OR CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0
```

Every package manifest in this repository should carry that expression, or a
single identifier from the list where the packaging format does not accept a
disjunction. You do not need permission to pick one: choose whichever member of
the list suits your project and comply with that one.

This applies to the whole repository: the README, the translations under
`locales/`, the specification, the website in `queue-theory.github.io/`, and
the documentation.
